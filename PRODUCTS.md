# MurhoPrints demo collection

Six hardcoded, unisex streetwear concepts. Each piece appears in both Women and Men collections and has S, M, L, and XL options. Prices and the 12-piece-per-size inventory are demonstration data, not live availability. Images are generated concept visuals.

| Concrete Hoodie | ₹2,999 | Olive | 400 GSM brushed cotton fleece; roomy hood, relaxed shape | `street-hoodie.png` |
| Daily Cargo | ₹2,499 | Stone | Cotton twill; relaxed hip, wide leg, cargo pockets | `street-cargo.png` |

| Studio Layer Set | ₹4,499 | Olive + charcoal | Cotton fleece hoodie and cotton twill trousers | `street-hoodie.png` |

The hoodie and layer set intentionally share a styled outfit image. The separate `street-fabric.png` supports the material story. The hero uses the seated model composition with an oversized tee and cargos.

Edit all product information in `js/data/products.js`. Product URLs use `/product/<id>`; IDs are readable names such as `/product/inkline-oversized-tee`. Care instructions and garment measurements appear on each detail page. There is no purchasing flow.
